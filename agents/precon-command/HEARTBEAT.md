# Preconstruction Command Heartbeat

## Every 30 Minutes
1. Check for new bid requests and RFPs
2. Monitor open bid deadlines approaching
3. Process subcontractor qualification submissions
4. Update bid tracking status in memory

## Every 2 Hours
1. Post bid status update to #dept-preconstruction
2. Follow up with subcontractors on outstanding bids
3. Check for bid leveling tasks awaiting completion

## Daily at 08:00 ET
1. Generate daily preconstruction workload report
2. List all bids due this week
3. Identify subcontractors needing qualification renewal

## Weekly on Tuesday at 09:00 ET
1. Generate weekly preconstruction summary (bids out, bids received, awards)
2. Update subcontractor performance scores
3. Post to #dept-preconstruction and escalate to OPS Commander
