# Subcontract Generation

## Purpose
Generate fully executed subcontract agreements from awarded bids using M4 Development Holdings LLC standard contract templates. Ensure all legal, insurance, and scope requirements are properly documented before work begins.

## Contracting Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Prime contractor on commercial and multi-family projects
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Prime on sitework packages or as sub to M4 GC
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential construction contracts

## Subcontract Generation Workflow

### Step 1: Award Notification
- Send formal award notification letter to the selected subcontractor via email.
- Include the awarded scope, contract amount, and anticipated start date.
- Request updated insurance certificates, W-9, and signed acknowledgment within 5 business days.
- Notify unsuccessful bidders with a professional decline communication.

### Step 2: Contract Assembly
- Select the correct template based on the contracting entity (M4 GC, Overland, or Southern View).
- Populate the agreement with: project name, address, owner, contract sum, scope of work, schedule, and retainage terms.
- Attach the following exhibits: scope of work from the awarded bid, project plans and specifications list, project schedule, safety requirements, and insurance requirements.
- Include state-specific provisions based on the project jurisdiction (NC, SC, FL, or AL).

### Step 3: Insurance and Compliance Verification
- Verify the subcontractor's insurance meets minimum requirements: $1M GL per occurrence, $2M aggregate, $1M auto, statutory workers' compensation.
- Confirm the contracting M4 entity is listed as Additional Insured on the GL policy.
- Verify current state contractor license for the applicable trade and jurisdiction.
- For projects over $100K, verify bonding capacity if a performance bond is required.

### Step 4: Contract Review and Approval
- Route the draft subcontract through the internal approval chain: Project Manager, Preconstruction Manager, and entity Principal.
- For contracts exceeding $250K, require additional review by legal counsel.
- Track approval status and maintain a log of all reviewer comments and revisions.
- Generate the final execution-ready PDF once all internal approvals are obtained.

### Step 5: Execution and Distribution
- Send the subcontract to the subcontractor for signature via DocuSign or wet signature.
- Set a 10-business-day deadline for return of signed documents.
- Upon full execution, distribute copies to: Project Manager, Accounting, and the subcontractor.
- Upload the executed subcontract to the project folder in Google Drive and Procore.
- Create the subcontract commitment in Procore for budget tracking purposes.

## Standard Contract Terms
- Retainage: 10% withheld until 50% complete, then reduced to 5% through substantial completion.
- Payment terms: Net 30 from receipt of approved invoice with proper lien waiver.
- Change order process: No work without written authorization; all changes require signed change order.
- Dispute resolution: Mediation followed by binding arbitration per the contract jurisdiction.

## State-Specific Requirements
- **NC**: Lien waiver format per NC GS 44A. Prompt payment per NC GS 22C-1.
- **SC**: Mechanic's lien provisions per SC Code 29-5. Payment bond requirements for public work.
- **FL**: Lien rights per FL Statute 713. Notice to Owner requirements must be addressed.
- **AL**: Mechanic's lien per AL Code 35-11. Contractor licensing per AL Code 34-8.

## Document Storage
- All executed subcontracts stored in: Google Drive > [Entity] > [Project] > Contracts > Subcontracts.
- Maintain a master subcontract log tracking: sub name, trade, amount, execution date, and status.
