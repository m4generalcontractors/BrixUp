# Bid Solicitation

## Purpose
Manage the creation and distribution of Requests for Proposal (RFPs) to qualified subcontractors across all M4 Development Holdings LLC entities. Enforce the company policy of obtaining a minimum of 3 bids per trade on every project.

## Operating Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - General contracting projects (NC, SC, FL, AL)
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Site development and earthwork scopes
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential construction projects

## Bid Solicitation Workflow

### Step 1: Scope Identification
- Review project plans and specifications to identify all trade scopes requiring subcontractor bids.
- Break scopes into CSI divisions (e.g., Division 03 Concrete, Division 07 Thermal/Moisture, Division 26 Electrical).
- Flag any specialty scopes that may have limited subcontractor availability in the project's jurisdiction.

### Step 2: Subcontractor Selection
- Pull from the approved subcontractor database organized by trade, state, and past performance rating.
- For each trade scope, identify a minimum of 5 subcontractors to invite (targeting 3+ returned bids).
- Verify each subcontractor's license status, insurance currency, and bonding capacity before invitation.
- Prioritize subcontractors with successful project history on similar M4 projects.

### Step 3: RFP Package Assembly
- Generate a bid package containing: invitation letter, scope of work narrative, relevant plan sheets, specifications, bid form template, and project schedule.
- Include project-specific requirements: insurance minimums, safety program requirements, prevailing wage applicability, and MBE/WBE goals if applicable.
- Attach the standard M4 subcontract terms and conditions for bidder review.
- Set a clear bid due date allowing a minimum of 10 business days for bidder response.

### Step 4: Distribution and Tracking
- Distribute RFPs via email with read receipts and log in the bid tracking spreadsheet.
- Send RFP packages through Procore's bidding module when available on the project.
- Track invitation status: Sent, Opened, Acknowledged, Declined, Submitted.
- Send reminder notifications at 5 days and 2 days before the bid deadline.

### Step 5: Bid Receipt and Compliance Check
- Log all received bids with timestamp, bidder name, and bid amount.
- Verify each bid includes all required documents: bid form, schedule, exclusions list, and insurance certificate.
- If fewer than 3 bids are received for any trade, extend the deadline or solicit additional subcontractors.
- Flag any bids that contain material scope exclusions or non-standard qualifications.

## Jurisdiction Notes
- **North Carolina**: Verify NC General Contractor or appropriate trade license.
- **South Carolina**: Verify SC Contractor License Board registration.
- **Florida**: Verify DBPR license and check for any disciplinary actions.
- **Alabama**: Verify Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors status.

## Compliance Requirements
- Maintain a complete bid log for every project as part of the permanent project record.
- All bid communications must be documented and stored in the project's Google Drive folder.
- Never share one subcontractor's pricing with another during the bidding process.
- Ensure fair and competitive bidding practices in compliance with company policy.
