# Inspection Scheduling

## Purpose
Schedule, track, and manage all required building inspections with local building departments for M4 Development Holdings LLC projects. Ensure inspections are requested on time, results are documented, and any failed inspections are addressed promptly.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Commercial and multi-family project inspections
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Erosion control, utility, and grading inspections
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential building inspections per lot

## Standard Inspection Sequence (Typical)
1. Erosion control / silt fence installation
2. Footing/foundation (before pour)
3. Foundation wall / slab (before backfill)
4. Rough framing / structural
5. Mechanical rough-in (HVAC ductwork)
6. Electrical rough-in
7. Plumbing rough-in
8. Insulation
9. Fire alarm / sprinkler (before concealment)
10. Drywall / lath (before finishing)
11. Final mechanical
12. Final electrical
13. Final plumbing
14. Fire final
15. Final building / Certificate of Occupancy

## Inspection Scheduling Workflow

### Step 1: Inspection Planning
- At project start, create an inspection schedule aligned with the construction schedule milestones.
- Identify all required inspections per the issued permits and the jurisdiction's inspection checklist.
- Map inspection requirements to schedule activities so inspections are requested at the correct time.
- Note jurisdiction-specific lead time requirements (most require 24-48 hours advance notice).

### Step 2: Inspection Request
- Request inspections through the jurisdiction's preferred method: online portal, phone call, or IVR system.
- Submit inspection requests with a minimum lead time of 48 hours (or per jurisdiction requirements).
- Include in the request: permit number, project address, inspection type, preferred date, and site contact.
- Log the inspection request in the project inspection tracking spreadsheet and Procore.
- Add the scheduled inspection to the project Google Calendar with attendee notifications to the superintendent.

### Step 3: Pre-Inspection Preparation
- The superintendent must conduct a pre-inspection walkthrough 24 hours before the scheduled inspection.
- Verify all work is complete, accessible, and code-compliant before the inspector arrives.
- Ensure required items are visible and accessible: labels, tags, fire stopping, structural connections, test reports.
- Have the approved plans, permit card, and any required test reports available on-site for the inspector.
- Confirm the site contact will be present to walk the inspector through the work.

### Step 4: Inspection Results Documentation
- Record inspection results immediately: Pass, Pass with Corrections, or Fail.
- Document the inspector's name, date, time, and any comments or correction items.
- For passed inspections, upload the signed inspection card or report to Google Drive and Procore.
- For failed inspections, document every deficiency noted by the inspector with photos.
- Update the inspection tracking log with results and any required re-inspection dates.

### Step 5: Failed Inspection Resolution
- Assign each deficiency to the responsible subcontractor or crew for immediate correction.
- Set a correction deadline not to exceed 3 business days (unless the correction requires material or design changes).
- Re-request the inspection once all corrections are verified complete by the superintendent.
- Track correction costs; if caused by a subcontractor's non-compliant work, issue a back-charge.
- Maintain a deficiency log showing the pattern of failed inspections by trade for performance tracking.

## Jurisdiction Scheduling Tips
- **NC**: Most jurisdictions use online portals (e.g., CityView, EnerGov). Schedule by 3 PM for next-day inspections.
- **SC**: Some counties require phone-in scheduling. Rural areas may have limited inspector availability.
- **FL**: Private provider inspections can expedite the process when the building department has long lead times.
- **AL**: Contact the county building department early to confirm inspector availability, as some rural counties have limited staff.

## Reporting
- Generate weekly inspection status reports showing upcoming, completed, and pending re-inspections.
- Track inspection pass rate by project, trade, and subcontractor for quality management.
- Coordinate inspection schedules with the project schedule to avoid delays on the critical path.
