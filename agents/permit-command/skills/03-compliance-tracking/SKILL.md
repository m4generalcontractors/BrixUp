# Compliance Tracking

## Purpose
Monitor and maintain license, insurance, and regulatory compliance for all M4 Development Holdings LLC entities, employees, and subcontractors. Ensure continuous compliance with federal, state, and local requirements across NC, SC, FL, and AL operations.

## Compliance Categories

### Entity Licenses and Registrations
- **M4 General Contractors**: General contractor licenses in NC, SC, FL, and AL.
- **Overland Grading & Sitework**: Grading contractor licenses and NPDES permits.
- **M4 Aggregates**: Mining permits, MSHA compliance, environmental permits.
- **Southern View Homes**: Residential contractor/builder licenses per state.
- **NexGen Capital Partners**: SEC and state securities registrations (if applicable).
- **BrixUp Technologies**: Business registrations and software licenses.
- All entities: State business registrations, annual report filings, and local business licenses.

### Insurance Compliance
- General Liability (minimum $1M/$2M per entity)
- Automobile Liability (minimum $1M per entity)
- Workers' Compensation (statutory limits per state)
- Umbrella/Excess Liability
- Builders Risk (project-specific)
- Professional Liability (for BrixUp Technologies)
- Directors & Officers (for NexGen Capital Partners)

### Subcontractor Compliance
- Valid state contractor license for the applicable trade and jurisdiction
- Current insurance certificates meeting M4 minimums with Additional Insured endorsement
- Workers' compensation coverage verification
- W-9 on file for 1099 reporting
- Signed master subcontract agreement
- Safety program documentation and EMR (Experience Modification Rate)

## Compliance Tracking Workflow

### Step 1: Compliance Database Setup
- Maintain a master compliance tracking spreadsheet (Google Sheets) with tabs for: Entity Licenses, Entity Insurance, Subcontractor Licenses, Subcontractor Insurance, and Regulatory Permits.
- Record for each item: document type, issuing authority, issue date, expiration date, renewal lead time, responsible party.
- Color-code by status: Green (current, 60+ days remaining), Yellow (expiring within 60 days), Red (expired or expiring within 30 days).

### Step 2: Automated Monitoring
- Set Google Calendar reminders at 90-day, 60-day, and 30-day intervals before each expiration.
- Send automated email notifications to the responsible party at each reminder interval.
- Escalate to entity leadership if any item reaches the 30-day warning without a renewal in progress.
- Run a weekly compliance dashboard report showing all items by status.

### Step 3: Renewal Processing
- Initiate renewal applications at least 60 days before expiration for all entity licenses.
- Request updated insurance certificates from the broker at least 30 days before policy renewal.
- For subcontractors, request updated documents 30 days before expiration with a firm deadline 15 days before.
- Verify renewed documents for accuracy: correct entity name, coverage amounts, additional insured endorsements.

### Step 4: Subcontractor Compliance Enforcement
- No subcontractor may begin work on any M4 project without current, verified compliance documents.
- If a subcontractor's insurance or license expires during a project, issue a 48-hour cure notice.
- Suspend the subcontractor's work if compliance is not restored within the cure period.
- Maintain a subcontractor compliance scorecard factoring into future bid invitation decisions.

### Step 5: Regulatory Compliance Monitoring
- Track OSHA requirements: 300 logs, safety training records, toolbox talk documentation.
- Monitor NPDES and erosion control permit compliance for all active sites.
- Track EPA and state environmental compliance for M4 Aggregates operations.
- Maintain DOT compliance for any entity-owned commercial vehicles and equipment transport.
- Monitor wage and hour compliance, including prevailing wage requirements on applicable projects.

## State-Specific License Requirements
- **NC**: NC Licensing Board for General Contractors (required for projects over $30,000).
- **SC**: SC Contractor Licensing Board (required for projects over $5,000).
- **FL**: DBPR Construction Industry Licensing Board (state-wide license required).
- **AL**: Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors (required for projects over $50,000).

## Audit and Reporting
- Conduct quarterly internal compliance audits across all entities.
- Generate monthly compliance status reports for entity leadership.
- Maintain an audit trail of all compliance verifications for legal and bonding purposes.
- Prepare annual compliance summaries for insurance renewal applications and bonding company reviews.
