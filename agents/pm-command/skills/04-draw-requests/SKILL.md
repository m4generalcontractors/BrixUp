# Draw Requests

## Purpose
Process monthly construction draw requests (pay applications) for all M4 Development Holdings LLC projects. Align billing with actual completion percentages, coordinate with lender requirements, and ensure timely cash flow for project operations.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - AIA G702/G703 pay applications to owners and lenders
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Progress billing for sitework contracts
- **Southern View Homes** - Construction loan draws for residential builds
- **NexGen Capital Partners** - Development fund draw coordination with construction lenders

## Draw Request Workflow

### Step 1: Percent Complete Assessment
- Walk the project with the superintendent to assess physical percent complete for every schedule of values (SOV) line item.
- Compare field-observed progress to the cost-loaded schedule for consistency.
- Review stored materials eligible for billing (materials on-site but not yet installed).
- Document the basis for percent complete with progress photos and daily log references.

### Step 2: Draw Preparation
- Update the schedule of values in Procore or Google Sheets with current percent complete per line item.
- Calculate the current billing amount: (Current % Complete x SOV Amount) - Previous Billings = Current Draw.
- Apply retainage per contract terms (typically 10% until 50% complete, then 5%).
- Include approved change orders in the billing with proper documentation.
- For AIA-format contracts, prepare G702 (Application and Certificate for Payment) and G703 (Continuation Sheet).

### Step 3: Supporting Documentation
- Compile all required supporting documents for the draw package:
  - Conditional lien waivers from all subcontractors for the current billing period.
  - Unconditional lien waivers from all subcontractors for the previous billing period.
  - Updated project schedule showing current progress.
  - Progress photos dated within the billing period.
  - Copies of approved change orders included in this draw.
  - Insurance certificates if expired or updated since last draw.
- For lender-funded projects, include the lender's required draw request form and inspection authorization.

### Step 4: Review and Submission
- Route the draft draw through internal review: PM, Senior PM, entity Controller.
- Submit the draw package to the owner/architect for review by the contractual submission deadline (typically the 25th of each month).
- For lender-funded projects, submit simultaneously to the construction lender with their required forms.
- Track submission and follow up on any requests for additional information within 48 hours.

### Step 5: Payment Tracking
- Log the submitted draw amount, submission date, and expected payment date.
- Track owner/lender review status: Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Funded.
- Follow up on any draws not funded within the contractual payment period (typically 30 days).
- Upon receipt, reconcile the payment to the draw amount and record in QuickBooks.
- Issue subcontractor payments within 7 days of receiving owner/lender funding per contract terms.

## Lender Draw Specifics
- Construction lenders typically require a third-party inspection before funding. Schedule inspections at least 5 days before the desired funding date.
- Maintain the lender's draw tracking spreadsheet showing cumulative draws against the total loan commitment.
- Track hard cost vs. soft cost draws separately as required by most construction loan agreements.
- Coordinate with NexGen Capital Partners for equity draw requirements on investor-funded projects.

## Retainage Management
- Track retainage withheld by the owner and retainage held from subcontractors separately.
- Process retainage release requests at substantial completion per contract terms.
- Ensure final lien waivers are obtained before releasing retainage to subcontractors.

## Monthly Draw Calendar
- **Day 1-20**: Field percent complete assessment and draw preparation.
- **Day 20-25**: Internal review and approval.
- **Day 25**: Submission to owner/lender.
- **Day 25-30**: Owner/lender review period and inspection coordination.
