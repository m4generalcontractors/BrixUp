# Schedule Management

## Purpose
Manage and maintain construction project schedules for all M4 Development Holdings LLC active projects. Track milestones, monitor the critical path, identify delays early, and coordinate schedule updates through Procore.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Commercial and multi-family project schedules
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Sitework and grading phase schedules
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential build schedules by lot and phase

## Schedule Management Workflow

### Step 1: Baseline Schedule Creation
- Develop the baseline CPM (Critical Path Method) schedule during preconstruction using project milestones from the owner contract.
- Break the schedule into phases: Mobilization, Sitework, Foundation, Structural, MEP Rough-In, Finishes, Commissioning, Closeout.
- Assign durations, predecessors, and successors for every activity.
- Identify the critical path and near-critical activities (less than 5 days float).
- Upload the baseline schedule to Procore and distribute to all subcontractors.

### Step 2: Weekly Schedule Updates
- Update actual start and finish dates for all in-progress and completed activities every Monday.
- Recalculate the critical path after each update and note any shifts.
- Compare current schedule to baseline, documenting any variance exceeding 3 days.
- Generate a 3-week look-ahead schedule for field distribution every week.
- Distribute the look-ahead to subcontractors, the project owner, and field superintendents.

### Step 3: Milestone Tracking
- Maintain a milestone dashboard showing key dates: permit issuance, foundation complete, dry-in/weathertight, rough-in inspections, substantial completion, and final completion.
- Track milestone variance against the baseline and contractual deadlines.
- Flag milestones at risk of delay 2+ weeks in advance so corrective action can be taken.
- Report milestone status to ownership and NexGen Capital Partners for investor-funded projects.

### Step 4: Delay Analysis and Recovery
- When delays occur, document the cause: weather, material delivery, subcontractor performance, design changes, or permit issues.
- Classify delays as excusable (weather, owner-caused) or non-excusable (subcontractor-caused).
- Develop recovery schedules showing acceleration options: overtime, additional crews, re-sequencing.
- Issue formal delay notices when contractual milestones are impacted.

### Step 5: Schedule Reporting
- Generate weekly schedule status reports for the project management team.
- Produce monthly executive schedule summaries for entity leadership.
- Maintain a master portfolio schedule view showing all active projects across M4 entities.
- Track earned schedule metrics: Schedule Performance Index (SPI) and Schedule Variance (SV).

## Procore Integration
- All schedules are maintained in Procore's Schedule tool as the system of record.
- Link schedule activities to Procore submittals, RFIs, and inspections for traceability.
- Use Procore's calendar view for field team access to upcoming activities.

## Weather Day Tracking
- Log weather delays per jurisdiction standards (NC, SC, FL, AL) with supporting weather data.
- Track cumulative weather days against the contract allowance.
- Coordinate weather delay documentation with the daily log workflow.
