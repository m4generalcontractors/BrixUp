# Daily Logs

## Purpose
Capture comprehensive field daily logs for every active construction project under M4 Development Holdings LLC. Daily logs serve as the official project record for weather conditions, manpower, equipment, work performed, safety observations, and issues encountered.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - All commercial and multi-family project sites
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - All grading and sitework operations
- **Southern View Homes** - All residential construction sites

## Daily Log Workflow

### Step 1: Morning Entry (By 9:00 AM)
- Record the date, project name, project number, and reporting superintendent.
- Log weather conditions: temperature (high/low), precipitation (type and amount), wind speed, and sky conditions.
- Record whether the day is a full work day, partial work day, or weather day.
- Note any safety toolbox talk topic conducted that morning.

### Step 2: Manpower and Equipment Tracking
- Log all subcontractor crews on-site with company name, trade, and headcount.
- Record M4/Overland self-perform crew members by name and role.
- List all major equipment on-site: cranes, excavators, loaders, concrete pumps, etc.
- Note any equipment mobilizations or demobilizations that occurred.
- Track total man-hours on-site for the day across all trades.

### Step 3: Work Completed
- Document all work activities performed by each trade in clear, specific language.
- Reference building areas, grid lines, floor levels, or lot numbers for location specificity.
- Note quantities installed where applicable: cubic yards of concrete, linear feet of pipe, square feet of drywall.
- Record any inspections conducted and their pass/fail status.
- Document material deliveries received with quantities and storage location.

### Step 4: Issues and Delays
- Record any work stoppages and their cause with start and end times.
- Document any safety incidents, near misses, or OSHA visit details.
- Log any RFIs generated from field conditions discovered during the day.
- Note any subcontractor performance issues: insufficient crew, quality deficiencies, schedule non-compliance.
- Record any visitor log entries: owner representatives, architects, engineers, inspectors.

### Step 5: Photo Documentation
- Attach a minimum of 5 progress photos per day to the daily log.
- Photos must include: overall site conditions, active work areas, material deliveries, and any issues or deficiencies.
- Use Procore's photo tool to tag photos with location, trade, and description.
- Ensure photos are timestamped and geotagged for record accuracy.

## Procore Integration
- All daily logs are entered directly into Procore's Daily Log module.
- Use Procore's manpower tracking for automated crew count reporting.
- Link daily log entries to schedule activities for progress correlation.
- Configure automatic distribution of daily logs to the Project Manager and ownership contacts.

## Compliance and Record Keeping
- Daily logs must be completed by end of business on the day of work. Late entries must note the actual entry date.
- Logs are legal documents and may be used in dispute resolution. Use factual, objective language only.
- Never delete or alter a submitted daily log. Use amendment entries for corrections.
- Maintain a continuous daily log record from Notice to Proceed through Final Completion.

## Reporting
- Generate weekly manpower summaries from daily log data for schedule and budget analysis.
- Produce monthly weather day summaries for contract time tracking.
- Aggregate daily log data for project closeout documentation packages.
