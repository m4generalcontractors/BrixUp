# Budget Tracking

## Purpose
Maintain real-time budget visibility for all active construction projects under M4 Development Holdings LLC. Track original budget, approved changes, committed costs, actual costs, and forecast cost-to-complete to ensure projects are delivered within financial targets.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Commercial and multi-family project budgets
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Sitework and self-perform cost tracking
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential lot-level budgets
- **NexGen Capital Partners** - Development-level budget oversight for investor-funded projects

## Budget Tracking Workflow

### Step 1: Budget Setup
- Establish the original project budget from the awarded contract amount and preconstruction estimate.
- Break the budget into cost codes aligned with CSI divisions and the company standard chart of accounts.
- Enter all subcontract commitments at their awarded amounts.
- Set up budget line items for general conditions, contingency, overhead, insurance, and fee.
- Load the budget into Procore and reconcile with the QuickBooks project job code.

### Step 2: Commitment Tracking
- Track all committed costs: subcontracts, purchase orders, and internal labor allocations.
- Record commitment changes (subcontract change orders) as they are approved.
- Monitor the gap between budgeted amounts and committed amounts to identify buy-out savings or overages.
- Maintain a buy-out log showing original budget vs. committed amount per trade.

### Step 3: Change Order Management
- Log all potential change orders (PCOs) as soon as they are identified in the field or through RFIs.
- Track PCO status: Identified, Priced, Submitted, Approved, Rejected.
- Maintain both owner change orders (revenue side) and subcontractor change orders (cost side).
- Ensure every cost-side change order has a corresponding owner-side change order or is absorbed by contingency.
- Route change orders through the approval chain: PM, Senior PM, entity Principal.

### Step 4: Cost-to-Complete Forecasting
- Update the cost-to-complete (CTC) forecast monthly for every budget line item.
- CTC formula: Original Budget + Approved Changes - Costs to Date = Projected Remaining Cost (adjusted for known conditions).
- Review each line item with the project superintendent and PM to identify emerging cost risks.
- Flag any line item where projected final cost exceeds the budget plus approved changes by more than 5%.
- Maintain a risk register of potential cost impacts not yet reflected in the forecast.

### Step 5: Reporting
- Generate monthly project cost reports showing: Original Budget, Approved Changes, Revised Budget, Committed, Costs to Date, CTC, and Projected Final Cost.
- Calculate key metrics: percentage complete (cost basis), contingency remaining, and projected margin.
- Produce entity-level portfolio budget summaries for leadership review.
- For NexGen-funded projects, generate investor-facing budget reports aligned with the capital stack.

## Integration Points
- **Procore**: System of record for budget, commitments, and change orders.
- **QuickBooks**: Sync actual costs (invoices paid) from QuickBooks to Procore monthly.
- **Google Sheets**: Supplemental analysis and executive dashboard views.

## Alerts and Thresholds
- Trigger alert when any line item exceeds 90% of its budget with work remaining.
- Trigger alert when total project contingency drops below 3% of contract value.
- Trigger alert when projected final cost exceeds the contract amount plus approved owner changes.
- Notify entity Principal immediately for any single change order exceeding $50K.

## Month-End Procedures
- Reconcile Procore budget with QuickBooks actual costs by the 5th of each month.
- Update CTC forecast by the 10th of each month for inclusion in entity financial reports.
- Archive monthly budget snapshot for historical trending and audit purposes.
