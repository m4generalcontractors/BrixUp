# Ad Campaign Management

## Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)
- Campaign objective: Lead Generation
- Target audiences: Homeowners 30-65, specific zip codes per state
- Budget management: Daily budget caps per campaign
- Ad formats: Carousel (project photos), Video (walkthroughs), Lead forms

## Google Ads
- Search campaigns: Construction-related keywords by region
- Local Services Ads: M4GC, Overland
- Display remarketing: Website visitors

## Campaign Monitoring
- Check ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) every 2 hours
- Pause campaigns with CPL > $100
- Scale campaigns with CPL < $40
- A/B test ad creative weekly

## Budget Alerts
- Daily spend threshold: $500/day across all campaigns
- Escalate to OPS Commander if daily spend exceeds $750
- Monthly budget cap: $15,000

## Reporting
- Cost per lead (CPL) by campaign and platform
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Conversion rate
- Lead quality score distribution
