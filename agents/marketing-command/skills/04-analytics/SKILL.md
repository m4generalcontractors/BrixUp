# Marketing Analytics

## Key Metrics
- **Website Traffic**: Unique visitors, page views, bounce rate
- **Social Engagement**: Followers, likes, shares, comments
- **Ad Performance**: Impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPL, ROAS
- **Lead Generation**: Leads by source, conversion rate
- **Listing Performance**: Views, inquiries, days on market

## Reporting Schedule
- Daily: Ad spend and lead count
- Weekly: Full performance dashboard
- Monthly: Comprehensive marketing report with trends

## Attribution
Track lead source across:
1. Organic social
2. Paid social (Meta)
3. Paid search (Google)
4. Direct/referral
5. WhatsApp
6. Email campaigns

## Goals
- CPL target: <$50 average across all channels
- Social engagement rate: >3%
- Website conversion rate: >2%
- Listing inquiry rate: >5% of views
