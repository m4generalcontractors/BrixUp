# Marketing Command Heartbeat

## Every 30 Minutes
1. Check social media engagement metrics across all platforms
2. Monitor active ad campaign performance (Meta/Google)
3. Process any new listing update requests
4. Update marketing metrics in memory

## Every 2 Hours
1. Post scheduled content to social channels
2. Analyze ad spend vs. lead generation ROI
3. Post performance snapshot to #dept-marketing

## Daily at 09:00 ET
1. Generate daily marketing performance report
2. Review and adjust ad campaign budgets
3. Check for new property listings needing marketing

## Daily at 17:00 ET
1. End-of-day engagement summary
2. Queue next day's social content
3. Flag underperforming campaigns for review

## Weekly on Monday at 09:00 ET
1. Generate weekly marketing report (reach, leads, spend, ROI)
2. Identify top-performing content and campaigns
3. Post to #dept-marketing and escalate to OPS Commander
