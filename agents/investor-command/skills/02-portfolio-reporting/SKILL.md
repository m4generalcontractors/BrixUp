# Portfolio Reporting

## Purpose
Generate comprehensive investor portfolio reports for NexGen Capital Partners. Calculate investment performance metrics, prepare distribution analyses, and deliver transparent reporting to Limited Partners on their real estate development investments with M4 Development Holdings LLC.

## Entity Context
- **NexGen Capital Partners** manages investment capital deployed across projects executed by M4 General Contractors, Overland Grading & Sitework, and Southern View Homes.
- Portfolio reports must reflect both individual deal performance and aggregate fund-level performance.
- Reports serve LPs, fund management, and regulatory/tax compliance purposes.

## Portfolio Reporting Workflow

### Step 1: Data Collection and Aggregation
- Gather current project data from each active investment across M4 entities:
  - Construction budget status (from Procore/budget tracking workflow)
  - Schedule status (percent complete, projected completion date)
  - Draw request history and remaining construction loan availability
  - Revenue projections (lease-up status, home sales pipeline, or disposition timeline)
- Collect fund-level financial data from QuickBooks: cash balances, expenses, management fees, and income.
- Reconcile all data sources to ensure consistency before report generation.

### Step 2: Performance Metric Calculation
- Calculate the following metrics for each investment and the overall portfolio:
  - **IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**: Based on actual cash flows to date and projected future cash flows.
  - **Equity Multiple**: Total projected distributions divided by total invested capital.
  - **Cash-on-Cash Return**: Annual cash distributions divided by invested equity.
  - **Preferred Return Accrual**: Track cumulative preferred return earned and paid per the Operating Agreement waterfall.
  - **Unrealized Value**: Current estimated market value of held assets minus outstanding debt.
- Compare actual performance against the original pro forma projections and note variances.

### Step 3: Distribution Waterfall Analysis
- Apply the fund's distribution waterfall per the Operating Agreement:
  - **Tier 1**: Return of invested capital to LPs.
  - **Tier 2**: Preferred return (typically 8-10% annualized) to LPs.
  - **Tier 3**: GP catch-up (if applicable).
  - **Tier 4**: Profit split between GP and LPs (typically 70/30 or 80/20).
- Calculate each investor's share of pending or projected distributions based on their pro-rata ownership.
- Maintain a distribution history log showing all prior distributions by investor, date, and amount.
- Model forward-looking distribution projections under base case, upside, and downside scenarios.

### Step 4: Report Generation
- Produce quarterly investor reports containing:
  - **Executive Summary**: Portfolio overview, key highlights, and any material updates.
  - **Project Updates**: Per-project status with construction progress, budget status, schedule, and photos.
  - **Financial Summary**: Fund-level P&L, balance sheet, and cash position.
  - **Performance Dashboard**: IRR, equity multiple, cash-on-cash, and comparison to pro forma.
  - **Capital Account Statement**: Each investor's individual capital account showing contributions, distributions, and current balance.
  - **Distribution Analysis**: Pending or projected distributions with waterfall calculations.
- Format reports professionally using the NexGen Capital Partners brand template.
- Include progress photos from active construction projects (sourced from the photo organization workflow).

### Step 5: Report Distribution and Follow-Up
- Deliver quarterly reports to all LPs within 45 days of quarter-end.
- Distribute via email with the full report attached as a PDF.
- Upload reports to each investor's secure folder on Google Drive.
- Schedule investor calls for any quarter with material variances (positive or negative) from projections.
- Log all investor inquiries received in response to reports and provide timely responses.
- Prepare annual tax packages (K-1 schedules) coordinated with the fund's CPA.

## Annual Reporting
- Deliver audited annual financial statements (if required by the Operating Agreement) within 90 days of year-end.
- Prepare the annual investor letter summarizing the year's performance, market outlook, and upcoming opportunities.
- Provide year-end capital account summaries and tax projections to investors before K-1 distribution.
- Conduct the Annual Meeting of Members (if required) with a prepared presentation of fund performance.

## Data Integrity and Controls
- All performance calculations must be independently verifiable with supporting data.
- Maintain version control on all distributed reports; archive each quarterly report in Google Drive.
- Use consistent valuation methodologies across reporting periods; document any methodology changes.
- Segregate investor data to ensure each LP only receives their own capital account information.
- Coordinate with the accounting team to ensure financial data used in reports matches the books of record.
