# Capital Raising

## Purpose
Manage the capital raising process for NexGen Capital Partners, the real estate investment arm of M4 Development Holdings LLC. Process investor interest, execute capital calls, track commitments, and coordinate fund administration for construction and development investment opportunities.

## Entity Context
- **NexGen Capital Partners** is the investment entity that raises capital from Limited Partners (LPs) to fund real estate development projects executed by M4 General Contractors, Overland Grading & Sitework, and Southern View Homes.
- Projects are typically structured as individual SPVs (Special Purpose Vehicles) or pooled funds.
- Investment opportunities span: ground-up commercial construction, residential subdivisions, land development, and value-add renovation projects across NC, SC, FL, and AL.

## Capital Raising Workflow

### Step 1: Investment Opportunity Packaging
- Receive a vetted development opportunity from the M4 development team with project pro forma.
- Prepare the investment summary package including: project overview, location analysis, market data, construction budget, projected timeline, capital stack, and target returns (IRR, equity multiple, cash-on-cash).
- Create the offering documents: Private Placement Memorandum (PPM), Subscription Agreement, Operating Agreement, and investor presentation deck.
- Define the capital raise target, minimum investment amount, and fundraising timeline.
- Store all offering documents in Google Drive under NexGen > [Fund/Deal Name] > Offering Documents.

### Step 2: Investor Outreach
- Identify target investors from the NexGen investor CRM (Google Sheets database).
- Segment investors by: accredited status, investment history, preferred deal size, and geographic interest.
- Distribute the investment summary to qualified investors via email with confidentiality notice.
- Schedule investor calls and presentations for interested parties.
- Track investor engagement: Contacted, Interested, Reviewing, Committed, Declined.

### Step 3: Subscription Processing
- Receive completed Subscription Agreements from committed investors.
- Verify accredited investor status with supporting documentation (income verification, net worth statement, or professional certification).
- Review subscription amounts against the fund's minimum and maximum thresholds.
- Log each subscription in the capital commitment tracker: investor name, entity, commitment amount, date signed, and anticipated funding schedule.
- Send acknowledgment of accepted subscription to the investor with next steps.

### Step 4: Capital Calls
- Issue capital call notices per the Operating Agreement terms (typically 10-15 business days advance notice).
- Specify in each capital call: total amount called, each investor's pro-rata share, wire instructions, and funding deadline.
- Track capital call responses: Notice Sent, Funds Received, Partial Payment, Default.
- Process incoming wires and match to investor accounts in QuickBooks.
- Issue capital call receipts and update the investor's funded capital balance.
- For defaulting investors, follow the remedies outlined in the Operating Agreement (penalty interest, dilution, or forfeiture).

### Step 5: Fund Administration
- Maintain the capitalization table showing each investor's commitment, funded amount, ownership percentage, and preferred return accrual.
- Track fund-level expenses: legal, accounting, management fees, and organizational costs.
- Coordinate with the accounting team for fund-level financial statement preparation.
- Manage investor communications: quarterly updates, capital call notices, distribution notices, and K-1 tax document distribution.
- Maintain the investor portal (Google Drive shared folders) with current fund documents and reports.

## Compliance and Legal
- All capital raising activities must comply with SEC Regulation D (typically Rule 506(b) or 506(c)) and applicable state securities laws.
- Verify all investors meet accredited investor requirements per SEC definitions.
- File Form D with the SEC within 15 days of the first sale of securities.
- File state Blue Sky notices as required in each state where investors reside.
- Maintain Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation for all investors.
- Retain all investor communications and subscription documents for a minimum of 7 years.

## Reporting Obligations
- Provide quarterly investor updates within 45 days of quarter-end.
- Deliver annual audited fund financial statements (if required by the Operating Agreement).
- Distribute K-1 tax documents to all investors by March 15 annually.
- Coordinate with the portfolio reporting workflow for investor-facing performance reports.
