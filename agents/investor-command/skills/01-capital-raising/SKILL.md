# Capital Raising

## NexGen Capital Partners Fund Management
Manage capital raising activities for NexGen Capital Partners (M4's investment arm).

## Investor Intake Process
1. Investor inquiry received (via Sales Command or direct)
2. Accreditation verification (income or net worth documentation)
3. Send offering documents and PPM
4. Schedule call with Miguel for high-value investors ($50K+)
5. Process subscription agreement
6. Collect capital commitment

## Capital Call Process
1. PM Command identifies project funding need
2. Calculate pro-rata allocation per investor
3. Send capital call notice (minimum 10 business days advance)
4. Track incoming wires/ACH
5. Confirm receipt and update portfolio records
6. Deploy capital to project account

## Investor Communication Templates
### Capital Call Notice
"Dear {name}, NexGen Capital Partners is issuing a capital call of ${amount} for the {project} project. Please wire funds by {date} to the account detailed in the attached instructions."

### Commitment Confirmation
"Thank you for your commitment of ${amount} to NexGen Capital Partners Fund {fund}. Your subscription has been accepted effective {date}."

## Compliance
- All communications must include required disclosures
- Track accredited investor status annually
- Maintain investor suitability documentation
- Never guarantee returns or use misleading language
