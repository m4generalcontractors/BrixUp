# Drawing Management

## Purpose
Manage construction drawing version control, distribution, and tracking for all M4 Development Holdings LLC construction projects. Ensure field teams always have access to the most current plan set and that superseded drawings are clearly marked.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Full plan sets for commercial and multi-family projects
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Civil, grading, and utility plan sets
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential plan sets by model and lot

## Drawing Management Workflow

### Step 1: Initial Plan Set Receipt and Setup
- Receive the issued-for-construction (IFC) plan set from the design team.
- Upload the complete plan set to the project's 02 - Plans & Specifications folder on Google Drive.
- Organize drawings by discipline: Architectural (A), Structural (S), Mechanical (M), Electrical (E), Plumbing (P), Civil (C).
- Upload to Procore's Drawings tool with proper sheet numbering and discipline tagging.
- Create a drawing log (Google Sheet) listing every sheet number, title, revision, and date.

### Step 2: Revision Processing
- When revised drawings are received (via ASI, bulletin, or reissue), log the revision immediately.
- Update the drawing log with: sheet number, new revision number/letter, date received, and description of changes.
- Upload the revised sheets to both Google Drive and Procore, replacing the previous version.
- Mark the previous version as SUPERSEDED (do not delete; maintain in a Superseded subfolder).
- Cloud or highlight the areas of change on the revised sheets when the design team has not already done so.

### Step 3: Distribution to Field
- Distribute updated drawings to the superintendent and relevant subcontractors within 24 hours of receipt.
- Use Procore's drawing distribution tool to push updates to field tablets and mobile devices.
- Send email notifications to all affected subcontractors listing the specific sheets revised and a summary of changes.
- For critical revisions (structural changes, MEP routing changes), schedule a coordination meeting.
- Obtain written acknowledgment from affected subcontractors that they have received and reviewed the revisions.

### Step 4: ASI and Bulletin Tracking
- Maintain an ASI (Architect's Supplemental Instructions) log tracking: ASI number, date, affected sheets, description, and cost impact.
- Track design bulletins and addenda in the same system.
- Coordinate ASI cost impacts with the budget tracking and change order workflows.
- Ensure all ASIs are cross-referenced in meeting minutes and RFI responses.

### Step 5: As-Built Drawing Management
- Maintain red-line as-built markups throughout construction on a dedicated set of field drawings.
- Collect as-built markups from all subcontractors (especially MEP trades) on a monthly basis.
- At project closeout, compile all as-built markups and submit to the design team for final as-built drawing preparation.
- Upload the final as-built drawings to the project's 12 - Closeout folder.

## Naming Convention
- Drawing files: [ProjectCode]_[Discipline]-[SheetNumber]_Rev[X]_[Date].pdf
- Example: M4GC2026003_A-201_RevB_20260315.pdf
- Plan sets: [ProjectCode]_FullSet_Rev[X]_[Date].pdf

## Version Control Rules
- Never overwrite a drawing file; always maintain the previous version in the Superseded folder.
- The Procore Drawings tool is the single source of truth for current drawing revisions.
- Field teams must confirm they are working from the current revision before starting new work areas.
- Conduct a monthly drawing currency audit to verify field sets match the current revision log.

## Southern View Homes Specifics
- Maintain a master plan library organized by home model/floor plan.
- Track lot-specific modifications (mirror plans, elevation changes, structural modifications).
- Distribute lot-specific plan sets to framing and trades crews before each lot start.
