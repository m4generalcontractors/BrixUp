# Photo Organization

## Purpose
Organize, tag, and maintain construction progress photos for all M4 Development Holdings LLC projects. Progress photos serve as critical project documentation for draw requests, dispute resolution, marketing, and project closeout.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Commercial and multi-family project photography
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Earthwork and sitework progress documentation
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential construction phase photography per lot

## Photo Organization Workflow

### Step 1: Photo Capture Standards
- Field personnel must capture a minimum of 5 progress photos per active project per day.
- All photos must be taken with timestamps and GPS geotagging enabled on the device.
- Required daily photo subjects: overall site conditions, each active work area, material deliveries, and any issues or deficiencies.
- Weekly required photos: aerial/drone overview (where available), safety conditions, and housekeeping.
- Milestone photos required at: pre-construction site conditions, foundation completion, structural framing, dry-in/weathertight, MEP rough-in, drywall, finishes, and substantial completion.

### Step 2: Upload and Initial Organization
- Upload all photos to Procore's Photos tool daily, tagged with the correct project.
- Procore auto-organizes by upload date; manually add location tags, trade tags, and album assignment.
- Create albums in Procore aligned with project phases: Pre-Construction, Sitework, Foundation, Structure, Rough-In, Finishes, Closeout.
- For Southern View Homes, create photo albums per lot number within each phase.

### Step 3: Tagging and Metadata
- Tag each photo with: project name, date, location (building/area/floor/lot), trade/discipline, and description.
- Use consistent location identifiers that match the project drawing grid lines or building designations.
- Tag photos related to issues or deficiencies with "ISSUE" and link to the corresponding RFI or punchlist item.
- Tag photos used for draw request support with "DRAW" and the draw period.

### Step 4: Google Drive Backup
- Sync all Procore project photos to the project's 09 - Photos folder on Google Drive weekly.
- Organize the Google Drive photos folder by month and phase:
  ```
  09 - Photos/
    2026-01 January/
    2026-02 February/
    Milestones/
    Aerials/
    Issues/
  ```
- Maintain a low-resolution archive for quick browsing and a full-resolution archive for official records.

### Step 5: Reporting and Distribution
- Generate weekly photo progress reports for owner and lender distribution.
- Compile monthly photo summaries for inclusion in project status reports.
- Prepare photo packages for draw request documentation showing work completed during the billing period.
- Create project completion photo portfolios for marketing use (with proper permissions).
- For NexGen Capital Partners investor-funded projects, include curated progress photos in quarterly investor reports.

## Photo Quality Standards
- Minimum resolution: 12 megapixels (standard smartphone camera quality or better).
- Photos must be well-lit and clearly show the subject matter.
- Include reference objects (tape measures, level rods) for photos documenting dimensions or elevations.
- Before/after photo pairs for all change order work and issue resolution.
- Avoid including personnel faces in photos used for external distribution without consent.

## Retention Policy
- Maintain all project photos for a minimum of 7 years after project completion.
- Store original unedited files; never replace originals with edited or cropped versions.
- Milestone and issue-related photos are part of the permanent project record.
- Aerial and drone footage follows the same retention schedule as standard progress photos.

## Overland Grading Specifics
- Capture GPS-tagged photos of existing site conditions before any earthwork begins.
- Document erosion control measures (silt fence, inlet protection) with dated photos per NPDES permit requirements.
- Photograph all underground utility installations before backfill with measurements visible.
