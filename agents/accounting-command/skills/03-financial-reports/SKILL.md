# Financial Reports

## Purpose
Generate accurate, timely financial reports for every M4 Development Holdings LLC entity. Deliver Profit & Loss statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow reports that support management decision-making, investor reporting, and tax preparation.

## Reporting Entities
Each entity produces standalone financial statements:
- **M4 Development Holdings LLC** - Consolidated holding company view
- **M4 General Contractors** - Construction operations
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Sitework operations
- **M4 Aggregates** - Materials supply operations
- **NexGen Capital Partners** - Investment fund reporting
- **Southern View Homes** - Residential construction
- **BrixUp Technologies** - Technology operations

## Financial Reports Workflow

### Step 1: Period Close Preparation
- Verify all transactions for the reporting period are categorized and posted in QuickBooks.
- Complete all inter-company transaction reconciliation to ensure eliminating entries are correct.
- Reconcile all bank accounts, credit cards, and loan accounts to statements.
- Review and post any required accruals: unbilled revenue, accrued expenses, depreciation.
- Verify payroll entries are posted and allocated to the correct entities and projects.

### Step 2: Profit & Loss Statement
- Generate the P&L for each entity showing: Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold, Gross Profit, Operating Expenses, and Net Income.
- For construction entities, break out revenue and COGS by project to show project-level profitability.
- Compare actuals to budget and to prior year same period.
- Calculate key metrics: gross margin percentage, operating margin, overhead rate.
- Highlight any unusual variances exceeding 10% from budget with explanatory notes.

### Step 3: Balance Sheet
- Generate the Balance Sheet for each entity showing: Assets, Liabilities, and Equity.
- Verify accounts receivable aging aligns with the AR detail report.
- Confirm accounts payable balances match vendor statements.
- Review work-in-progress (WIP) balances for construction entities using the percentage-of-completion method.
- Ensure inter-company receivables and payables balance across all entities.

### Step 4: Cash Flow Report
- Generate the Statement of Cash Flows for each entity: Operating, Investing, and Financing activities.
- Track cash position across all entity bank accounts.
- Produce a 13-week rolling cash flow forecast for each entity.
- Identify upcoming large cash needs: subcontractor payments, loan payments, equipment purchases, tax payments.
- Flag any entity projected to have less than 30 days of operating cash within the forecast period.

### Step 5: Consolidated Reporting
- Prepare consolidated financial statements for M4 Development Holdings LLC.
- Eliminate all inter-company transactions and balances in the consolidation.
- Generate a consolidated dashboard showing all entity performance side by side.
- Produce NexGen Capital Partners fund-level financial reports per the operating agreement requirements.
- Prepare investor-facing financial summaries for quarterly distribution.

## Report Distribution
- Monthly entity financial statements delivered to entity Principals by the 15th of the following month.
- Quarterly consolidated financials delivered to Holdings leadership by the 20th following quarter-end.
- NexGen investor reports delivered per the fund's reporting schedule (typically quarterly).
- Annual financial packages prepared for CPA firm review/audit by February 28th.

## Construction-Specific Reporting
- WIP (Work in Progress) schedule: Show project-level overbillings and underbillings.
- Job cost detail reports by project and cost code.
- Backlog report showing remaining contract value across all active projects.
- Bonding capacity utilization report for surety relationship management.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Revenue by entity (monthly and YTD)
- Gross margin by entity and by project
- Overhead rate as percentage of revenue
- AR aging and DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)
- Cash position and runway by entity
- Backlog and pipeline value for construction entities
