# Invoicing

## Purpose
Create, send, and track invoices for all M4 Development Holdings LLC entities. Manage accounts receivable through QuickBooks and Stripe to ensure timely collection and accurate revenue recognition.

## Entity-Specific Invoicing

### M4 General Contractors
- Progress billing (AIA G702/G703 format) to project owners and general contractors.
- Change order billing with supporting documentation.
- Retainage invoices at substantial completion milestones.

### Overland Grading & Sitework
- Unit price invoicing for earthwork (cubic yards moved, tons hauled).
- Lump sum progress billing for contracted sitework packages.
- T&M (Time and Materials) invoicing for change order work with daily tickets.

### M4 Aggregates
- Material delivery invoices based on scale tickets (tons delivered).
- Trucking and hauling invoices based on load count and distance.
- Monthly account statements for repeat customers.

### Southern View Homes
- Construction loan draw invoices per lot and phase.
- Home sale closing invoices coordinated with title companies.
- Warranty service invoices for out-of-warranty repair requests.

### NexGen Capital Partners
- Management fee invoices to fund entities.
- Development fee invoices to project-level SPVs.
- Investor capital call notices (coordinated with the capital raising workflow).

### BrixUp Technologies
- Software licensing and subscription invoices.
- Professional services and consulting invoices.

## Invoicing Workflow

### Step 1: Invoice Generation
- Select the correct entity and customer in QuickBooks.
- Enter line items with descriptions, quantities, rates, and applicable tax.
- For construction progress billing, reference the approved draw request or pay application.
- Apply the correct payment terms per the customer contract (Net 30, Net 45, etc.).
- Attach supporting documentation: lien waivers, daily tickets, scale tickets, or change order approvals.

### Step 2: Review and Approval
- Invoices over $25,000 require Project Manager review before sending.
- Invoices over $100,000 require entity Principal approval.
- Verify the invoice matches the contract terms, schedule of values, and approved amounts.
- Ensure proper tax treatment based on the jurisdiction and service type.

### Step 3: Distribution
- Send invoices via QuickBooks email or Stripe invoicing depending on the customer's preference.
- For AIA-format construction billing, send PDF packages via email with the full draw documentation.
- Set up Stripe payment links for customers who prefer online payment.
- Log the invoice send date and expected payment date in the AR tracking sheet.

### Step 4: Collections and Follow-Up
- Monitor aging receivables weekly: Current, 30-day, 60-day, 90-day buckets.
- Send automated payment reminders at 7 days past due and 30 days past due.
- Escalate invoices over 45 days past due to the entity Principal for direct follow-up.
- For construction retainage receivables, track against project closeout milestones.
- Document all collection communications for potential lien or legal action support.

### Step 5: Payment Recording
- Record payments received in QuickBooks matched to the correct invoice.
- Reconcile Stripe payments with QuickBooks entries daily.
- Apply partial payments correctly and update the remaining balance.
- For construction progress billing, track retainage withheld as a separate receivable.

## Compliance
- All invoices must include the entity's legal name, address, and contractor license number (where applicable).
- Maintain invoice numbering sequences per entity to prevent duplicates.
- Archive all invoices and supporting documents in Google Drive by entity and project.
