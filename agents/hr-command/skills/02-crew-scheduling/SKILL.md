# Crew Scheduling

## Purpose
Plan and manage daily crew deployment across all active M4 Development Holdings LLC construction projects. Optimize labor utilization by matching available crews to project needs based on skills, certifications, equipment requirements, and schedule priorities.

## Applicable Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Self-perform crews (concrete, carpentry, general labor)
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Equipment operators, laborers, grade crews
- **M4 Aggregates** - Plant operators, truck drivers, maintenance crews
- **Southern View Homes** - Production framing crews, finish crews, punch-out crews

## Crew Scheduling Workflow

### Step 1: Weekly Planning (Every Thursday for the Following Week)
- Review the 3-week look-ahead schedule for all active projects across every entity.
- Identify daily crew and equipment needs per project for the upcoming week.
- Collect crew requests from each project superintendent by Wednesday end of day.
- Cross-reference available crew members against the needs, accounting for PTO, training days, and medical restrictions.
- Identify any crew shortages and initiate temporary labor requests or inter-project transfers.

### Step 2: Daily Crew Assignment
- Finalize next-day crew assignments by 3:00 PM each day.
- Assign crew members to specific projects with: project name, address, superintendent name, report time, and scope of work for the day.
- For Overland, assign specific equipment (excavator, dozer, loader, haul truck) to each crew with the project assignment.
- Send daily crew deployment sheets to all superintendents and foremen by 4:00 PM via email or text.
- Notify any crew members being reassigned to a different project from the weekly plan.

### Step 3: Skill and Certification Matching
- Maintain a crew member profile database tracking: name, entity, trade skills, equipment certifications, OSHA certifications, driver's license class, and project history.
- Match crew assignments to skill requirements: certified operators for specific equipment, licensed trades for jurisdictional requirements.
- Ensure every active project has at least one crew member with a current First Aid/CPR certification.
- For projects requiring specialized certifications (crane operator, CDL, confined space), verify credentials before assignment.

### Step 4: Real-Time Adjustments
- Monitor daily attendance by 7:00 AM through superintendent check-in reports.
- When crew members call out, reassign from lower-priority projects or deploy standby personnel.
- Track weather impacts across all project locations; redeploy indoor-capable crews when exterior work is rained out.
- Coordinate emergency crew mobilization for time-sensitive activities (concrete pours, inspections, owner deadlines).
- Log all schedule changes and the reason for the adjustment in the crew tracking system.

### Step 5: Reporting and Optimization
- Generate weekly crew utilization reports showing: total available man-hours, deployed man-hours, utilization percentage, and idle time by entity.
- Track crew productivity metrics by project and work type (e.g., cubic yards per crew-day for Overland).
- Report overtime hours by crew member and project for cost control and labor compliance.
- Identify recurring shortages by trade or skill type to feed into the recruiting workflow.
- Produce monthly labor cost reports by project and entity for budget tracking alignment.

## Crew Deployment Rules
- No crew member may work more than 12 hours in a single shift without superintendent and HR approval.
- Overtime (over 40 hours/week) requires PM approval for the billing project.
- Travel time exceeding 1 hour each way to a project qualifies for per diem per company policy.
- Inter-entity crew sharing (e.g., Overland crew on an M4 GC project) must be documented with inter-company labor billing.
- All crew members must complete the project-specific safety orientation before starting work on a new site.

## Equipment Coordination (Overland and M4 Aggregates)
- Schedule equipment moves between projects with a minimum 48-hour lead time.
- Coordinate low-boy transport for equipment mobilization and demobilization.
- Track equipment hours per project for cost allocation and maintenance scheduling.
- Ensure equipment inspections are current before deploying to any project site.

## Integration Points
- Feed crew deployment data into daily logs for accurate manpower reporting.
- Sync crew schedules with project schedules to verify labor loading matches planned activities.
- Coordinate with the budget tracking workflow for labor cost forecasting.
