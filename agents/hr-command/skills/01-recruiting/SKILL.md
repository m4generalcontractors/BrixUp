# Recruiting

## Purpose
Manage the full recruiting lifecycle for all M4 Development Holdings LLC entities, from job posting through onboarding. Handle hiring for field trades, project management, office staff, and specialized roles across NC, SC, FL, and AL operations.

## Hiring Entities
- **M4 General Contractors** - Project managers, superintendents, estimators, office staff
- **Overland Grading & Sitework** - Equipment operators, laborers, foremen, grade checkers
- **M4 Aggregates** - Plant operators, truck drivers, equipment maintenance technicians
- **Southern View Homes** - Production managers, warranty technicians, sales staff
- **NexGen Capital Partners** - Analysts, fund administrators, investor relations
- **BrixUp Technologies** - Software developers, IT support, product managers

## Recruiting Workflow

### Step 1: Position Request and Approval
- Receive a hiring request from the entity manager specifying: role title, department, reporting structure, required start date, and compensation range.
- Verify the position is budgeted in the entity's current staffing plan.
- For new (unbudgeted) positions, route the request to entity Principal for approval before proceeding.
- Create the position record in the recruiting tracker (Google Sheets) with a unique requisition number.

### Step 2: Job Posting Creation
- Draft the job posting using entity-specific templates tailored to the role type (field, office, management).
- Include: job title, entity name, location, responsibilities, required qualifications, preferred qualifications, physical requirements, and compensation range.
- For field trades, emphasize: specific equipment/skills required, expected project types, travel requirements, and per diem/benefits.
- Add M4's standard EEO statement and benefits summary to every posting.

### Step 3: Job Distribution
- Post positions on the appropriate platforms based on role type:
  - **Field trades**: Indeed, ZipRecruiter, local trade unions, workforce development boards, Craigslist (by market).
  - **Project management/office**: Indeed, LinkedIn, industry associations (AGC, ABC, NAHB).
  - **Technology roles**: LinkedIn, Indeed, AngelList, Stack Overflow.
  - **Executive/specialized**: LinkedIn, industry recruiters, professional networks.
- Share postings internally across all M4 entities for employee referral opportunities.
- Track posting dates and platforms in the recruiting tracker.

### Step 4: Applicant Screening
- Review incoming applications daily and log each applicant in the tracking spreadsheet.
- Screen for minimum qualifications: required licenses/certifications, experience level, geographic availability.
- For field trades, verify: OSHA 10/30 certification, valid driver's license, equipment operation certifications.
- Conduct initial phone screens (15-20 minutes) for candidates meeting minimum qualifications.
- Score applicants on a standardized rubric and advance top candidates to interviews.

### Step 5: Interview and Selection
- Schedule interviews via Google Calendar with the hiring manager and relevant team members.
- For field positions, include a practical skills assessment or equipment operation demonstration.
- For office/management positions, conduct a two-round interview: HR screen and hiring manager interview.
- Conduct reference checks (minimum 2 professional references) for all final candidates.
- Run background checks and drug screening per M4 company policy before extending offers.
- Prepare and send offer letters using entity-specific templates. Track acceptance within 5 business days.

### Step 6: Onboarding Handoff
- Upon acceptance, initiate the onboarding checklist: I-9 verification, W-4, direct deposit, benefits enrollment, safety orientation scheduling.
- Assign new hires to an initial project or team and notify the receiving supervisor.
- For field employees, schedule OSHA orientation, company safety program review, and equipment familiarization.
- Add the new hire to the appropriate crew scheduling system.

## Recruiting Metrics
- Track time-to-fill by position type and entity.
- Monitor application volume and source effectiveness by platform.
- Report on offer acceptance rate and first-90-day retention rate.
- Maintain a pipeline dashboard showing open positions, active candidates, and pending offers across all entities.

## Compliance
- Maintain compliance with EEOC, ADA, and state employment laws across NC, SC, FL, and AL.
- Document all hiring decisions with objective, job-related criteria.
- Retain all application materials for a minimum of 2 years per EEOC guidelines.
- Verify employment eligibility (I-9) within 3 business days of hire date.
