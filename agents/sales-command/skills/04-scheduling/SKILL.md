# Appointment Scheduling

## Google Calendar Integration
Use Google Calendar API to manage appointments:
- Check availability before scheduling
- Send calendar invitations to clients
- Set reminders (24h and 1h before)

## Appointment Types
1. **Phone Consultation** - 30 min, no travel
2. **Site Visit** - 1 hour + travel time based on location
3. **Office Meeting** - 1 hour at M4 office
4. **Estimate Walkthrough** - 1.5 hours on-site

## Scheduling Rules
- Business hours: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM ET, Mon-Fri
- Saturday appointments available by request (9:00 AM - 2:00 PM)
- Minimum 2 hours between site visits (travel buffer)
- No double-booking Miguel's calendar

## Confirmation Flow
1. Propose 2-3 time slots to client
2. Confirm via client's preferred channel
3. Send calendar invite with address and contact info
4. Send reminder 24 hours before
5. Send reminder 1 hour before
