**1.2 Purpose.** The SPV exists exclusively to:
- (a) Acquire the Property at [PURCHASE PRICE]
- (b) Renovate the Property according to the approved Scope of Work
- (c) Sell or refinance the Property upon completion
- (d) Distribute proceeds to Members according to this Agreement

**1.3 Single-Purpose Entity.** The SPV shall not engage in any business activity other than the acquisition, renovation, and disposition of the Property.

**1.4 Platform Integration.** The SPV operates in conjunction with BrixUp Technologies LLC's platform and the Brix Box smart contract deployed at [CONTRACT ADDRESS] on the Base L2 network.

### Article 2: Members and Capital Contributions

**2.1 Managing Member.** [DEALMAKER NAME / ENTITY] shall serve as Managing Member with [X]% membership interest, responsible for day-to-day operations, contractor management, and property disposition.

**2.2 Investor Members.** The following individuals/entities are Investor Members:

| Member | Contribution | Interest % | Type |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|
| [Investor 1] | $[AMOUNT] USDC | [X]% | Cash Equity |
| [Investor 2] | $[AMOUNT] USDC | [X]% | Cash Equity |
| [Builder] | [HOURS] labor hours | [X]% | Sweat Equity |
| [Dealmaker] | Deal Origination | [X]% | Origination Equity |

**2.3 Sweat Equity Members.** Members contributing labor ("Sweat Equity Members") receive membership interests in exchange for labor commitments as defined in the Contractor Participation Agreement. Sweat Equity interests vest upon verified completion of labor milestones.

**2.4 Capital Calls.** Additional capital calls, if necessary, shall be approved by Members holding [66.7]% of membership interests. Members who do not participate in capital calls shall be diluted proportionally.

### Article 3: Management

**3.1** The SPV shall be manager-managed. The Managing Member has authority to:
- (a) Execute purchase and sale contracts for the Property
- (b) Approve draw requests and milestone verifications
- (c) Engage contractors, inspectors, and service providers
- (d) Make day-to-day operational decisions within the approved budget

**3.2** The following actions require approval of Members holding [51]% of interests:
- (a) Budget increases exceeding [10]% of original scope
- (b) Timeline extensions exceeding [30] days
- (c) Change of contractor or builder
- (d) Sale price acceptance below [90]% of projected ARV

**3.3** The Managing Member shall provide monthly progress reports to all Members through the BrixUp platform.

### Article 4: Distributions

**4.1 Distribution Waterfall.** Upon sale or refinance of the Property, net proceeds shall be distributed in the following order:

1. **Return of Capital:** Each Investor Member receives their original capital contribution
2. **Platform Fees:** BrixUp Technologies LLC origination and processing fees as defined in the Platform Services Agreement
3. **Preferred Return:** Investor Members receive a [8-12]% annualized preferred return on contributed capital
4. **Profit Split:** Remaining proceeds distributed according to membership interests:
   - Investor Members: [X]% of remaining profits, pro rata
   - Builder (Sweat Equity): [X]% of remaining profits
   - Managing Member (Dealmaker): [X]% of remaining profits

**4.2 Smart Contract Distribution.** Distributions shall be executed through the Brix Box smart contract, with the Managing Member depositing sale proceeds as USDC into the contract for automated distribution.

**4.3 Tax Distributions.** The SPV shall make reasonable tax distributions to Members to cover estimated tax liabilities from SPV income.

### Article 5: Draw Schedule and Construction Management

**5.1** Construction funding shall be disbursed according to the Draw Schedule Protocol defined in the Brix Box smart contract:

| Milestone | % of Budget | Amount | Verification |
|-----------|------------|--------|--------------|
| Foundation / Demolition | 15% | $[AMOUNT] | Permit + inspection |
| Framing / Structural | 25% | $[AMOUNT] | Photo + inspection |
| MEP Rough-In | 20% | $[AMOUNT] | Permit + inspection |
| Finishes | 30% | $[AMOUNT] | Photo + walkthrough |
| Certificate of Occupancy | 10% | $[AMOUNT] | CO document |

**5.2** Draw requests require multi-sig approval (2 of 3: platform admin, inspector, Managing Member).

### Article 6: Term and Dissolution

**6.1** The SPV shall dissolve upon the earlier of:
- (a) Distribution of all proceeds from the sale or refinance of the Property
- (b) [24 months] from the date of formation
- (c) Vote of Members holding [75]% of interests

**6.2** Upon dissolution, remaining assets shall be distributed according to the waterfall in Article 4.

### Article 7: Tax Matters

**7.1** The SPV shall be treated as a partnership for federal income tax purposes.

**7.2** The Managing Member shall serve as Tax Matters Partner.

**7.3** The SPV shall provide Schedule K-1s to all Members within [90] days of fiscal year end.

### Article 8: Governing Law

**8.1** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina.
